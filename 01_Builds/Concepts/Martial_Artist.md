# Martial Artist (Monk Ascendancy)

## Overview
* **Class:** Monk
* **Ascendancy:** Martial Artist
* **Core Skill:** Unarmed Attacks, Mirror Images
* **Playstyle:** Melee / Evasion

## Goals
- Master the **Mirror Images** mechanic to create offensive decoys.
- Utilize stone-infused glove buffs for massive physical damage.

## Gem Links
* **Main Skill:** Stone Fist, Mirror Strike
* **Auras/Buffs:** Spirit Trance
* **Movement:** Flying Kick
* **Utility:** Focus Balance

## Gear
* **Weapon:** Unarmed (Glove-focused)
* **Armour:** Evasion / Stone Skin (Runic Ward variants)

## Notes & Ideas
- High reliance on glove quality/enchants.
- Mirror Images benefit from "Increase Effect of Buffs" nodes.
