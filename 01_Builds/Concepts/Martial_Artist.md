# Martial Artist (Unarmed Monk Concept)

## Overview
* **Class:** Monk
* **Ascendancy:** [[Martial_Artist]] (System File)
* **Core Skill:** Unarmed Attacks, Mirror Images
* **Playstyle:** Melee / Evasion / Unarmed

## Goals
- Master the **Mirror Images** mechanic to create offensive decoys.
- Utilize **Fist of Stone** glove buffs for massive physical damage.
- Explore **Runic Meridian** for gear flexibility.

## Gem Links
* **Main Skill:** Stone Fist, Mirror Strike
* **Auras/Buffs:** Spirit Trance, Precision
* **Movement:** Flying Kick
* **Utility:** Focus Balance

## Gear
* **Weapon:** Unarmed (Glove-focused via Fist of Stone)
* **Armour:** Evasion / Runic Ward variants

## Notes & Ideas
- High reliance on glove quality/enchants.
- Mirror Images benefit from "Increase Effect of Buffs" nodes.
- Potential synergy with **Hollow Palm**.
