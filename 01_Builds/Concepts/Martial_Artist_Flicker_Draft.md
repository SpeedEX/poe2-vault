# Flicker Strike Martial Artist (Monk)

## Overview
* **Class:** Monk
* **Ascendancy:** [[Martial_Artist]]
* **Core Skill:** Flicker Strike
* **Playstyle:** Melee / Melee Unarmed (Hollow Palm Style)

## Goals
* Use **Fist of Stone** to transform Glove power into damage for unarmed combat (Potential Hollow Palm synergy).
* Solve Flicker Strike's Power Charge issues using **The Bell (Hollow Focus)** system.
* Utilize **Hollow Resonance** to create AOE Shockwaves while Flickering for clearing.
* **Charge Sustain:** Use **Siphoning Strike** on the bell to generate Power Charges for smooth single-target/boss encounters.

## Gem Links
* **Main Skill:** Flicker Strike
* **Support:** Siphoning Strike, Melee Physical Damage, Multistrike, Charge Regulation
* **Auras/Buffs:** Herald of Ash, Precision
* **Movement:** Flying Kick / Leap Slam
* **Utility:** Hollow Focus (The Bell)

## Gear
* **Weapon:** Unarmed (Hollow Palm)
* **Shield/Offhand:** None (Unarmed)
* **Armour:** Evasion Based / Runic Ward
* **Gloves:** High Flat Damage, Attack Speed, Crit (Transformed via Fist of Stone)
* **Jewels:** **Idol of Sirius** (Socketed in Gloves via Runic Meridian for +8% Attack Speed)

## Notes & Ideas
* **The Bell Strategy:** The bell from Hollow Focus can be "Culled" by Siphoning Strike, ensuring guaranteed Power Charge generation.
* **Charge Regulation:** Boosts Crit Chance and Attack Speed based on the number of Charges.
* **Runic Meridians:** Helps fix the damage loss from not using a passive weapon by allowing additional Runes in armor pieces.
