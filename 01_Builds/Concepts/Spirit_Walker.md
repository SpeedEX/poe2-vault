# Spirit Walker (Huntress Ascendancy)

## Overview
* **Class:** Huntress
* **Ascendancy:** Spirit Walker
* **Core Skill:** Wisp Buffs, Tame Beast
* **Playstyle:** Minion/Buffer Hybrid

## Goals
- Explore the **Tame Beast** mechanic for capturing and utilizing animal bosses in combat.
- Maximize **Wisp** uptime for self and minion buffs.

## Gem Links
* **Main Skill:** Tame Beast (Capturing), Spirit Strike (Direct Damage)
* **Auras/Buffs:** Wisp Infusion
* **Movement:** Wild Dash
* **Utility:** Animal Call

## Gear
* **Weapon:** Bow or Spear
* **Armour:** Evasion/Runic Ward Hybrid

## Notes & Ideas
- Synergizes well with [[Runic_Ward]] for survivability while minions tank.
- Capture specific animal bosses for targeted utility (e.g., speed, damage, or CC).
