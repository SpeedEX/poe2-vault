# Spirit Walker (Huntress Ascendancy)

## Overview
* **Class:** Huntress
* **Ascendancy:** [[Spirit_Walker]]
* **Core Skill:** Wisp Buffs, Tame Beast
* **Playstyle:** Minion/Buffer Hybrid

## Goals
- Explore the **Tame Beast** mechanic for capturing and utilizing animal bosses (e.g., *Silberfaust*, *Chimera*) in combat.
- Maximize **Holy Wisps** uptime for self and minion buffs.

## Gem Links
* **Main Skill:** Tame Beast (Capturing), Spirit Strike (Direct Damage)
* **Auras/Buffs:** Wisp Infusion (Deer/Owl/Bear variants)
* **Movement:** Wild Dash
* **Utility:** Animal Call

## Gear
* **Weapon:** Bow or Spear
* **Armour:** Evasion/Runic Ward Hybrid
* **Accessories:** **Idols** (enhanced by *Idol Worship*)

## Notes & Ideas
- Synergizes well with [[Runic_Ward]] for survivability.
- Combine all three wisp types to unlock the powerful **Holy Wisps** passive.
- Tame specific bosses for targeted utility (e.g., Bear for tanking/regen, Deer for aggression).
