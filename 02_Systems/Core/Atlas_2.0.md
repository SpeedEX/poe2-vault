# Atlas 2.0 (Endgame)

The "Origins of Divinity" expansion introduces a major overhaul to the Atlas of Worlds, focusing on progression flexibility and targeted farming.

## 🏰 The Fortress (Citadel)
- **New Zones:** Massive precursor structures appearing in the Atlas.
- **Pinnacle Encounters:** Defeat ancient bosses to unlock exclusive Atlas Passive points.

## 🌳 Passive Tree Overhaul
- **Full Allocation:** Players can eventually allocate every node on the Atlas tree through dedicated grinding.
- **Multi-Choice Nodes:** Key Notable nodes now feature 2-3 optional bonuses, allowing players to tailor the content (e.g., choosing between more Ritual rewards vs. faster Ritual encounters).

## 🧭 Masters of the Atlas
- Masters can now be assigned to specific regions.
- Provides static, predictable buffs to maps within those regions.
