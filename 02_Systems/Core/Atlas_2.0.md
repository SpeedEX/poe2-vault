# Atlas 2.0 (Endgame)

The "Origins of Divinity" expansion introduces a major overhaul to the Atlas of Worlds, focusing on progression flexibility and targeted farming.

## 🌳 Origins of Divinity Arc
- **Lore:** Explore the makers of the **Third Edict** (the Precursors) guided by the thaumaturge **Doryani**.
- **Progression:** Fixed Atlas layout. Completing a map that gives a passive point completes it for everyone in the party. (Exception: Grand Mirror rewards are individual).
- **Geography:** Physical geography matches Wraeclast's map. Leagues are placed geographically.
- **Completion:** Pinnacle boss via shortest path takes approx 40-50 maps. Total atlas tree has 300+ nodes; fortress completion requires ~150 maps.

## 🏰 The Fortress (Citadel)
- **Mechanics:** 15 new bosses in 0.5. Infinite attempts for pinnacle bosses once unlocked.
- **League Placement:** Breach is closest to the start (4-5 maps). Each league quest line takes roughly one solid night of gaming.

## 🌳 Passive Tree & Masters
- **Masters of the Atlas:**
    - **Jado**: Discover powerful artefacts.
    - **Hilda**: Hunt deadly monsters.
    - **Doryani**: Cleanse the corruption (**Doryani's Science**). Became an Atlas master.
    - **Runesmith NPC**: Possible future master if Runes of Aldur goes core.
- **Progression:** Each master has four rows of abilities, unlocked by completing missions.

## ⚖️ Endgame Philosophy
- **Design:** Added "quitting points" (progression bars, quest endings) to prevent player burnout.
- **Rarity/Economy:** GG aims to remove rarity eventually; meanwhile, leagues have distinct reward profiles to allow targeted farming.
