# Atlas 2.0 (Endgame)

The "Origins of Divinity" expansion introduces a major overhaul to the Atlas of Worlds, focusing on progression flexibility and targeted farming.

## 🌳 Origins of Divinity Arc
- **Lore:** Explore the makers of the **Third Edict** (the Precursors) guided by the thaumaturge **Doryani**.
- **Progression:** Triggered by completing the first **Atlas Tower**.
- **Fixed Points:** Guaranteed spawn locations across 30+ new maps.
- **The Fortress:** Reach the heart of the fortress to face **The Ordained**.

## 🏰 The Fortress (Citadel)
- **New Zones:** Massive precursor structures appearing in the Atlas.
- **Map Modifiers:** Includes **Azmeri Spirits** that move to new hosts upon defeat for chain rewards.

## 🌳 Passive Tree & Masters
- **Full Allocation:** Players can eventually allocate every node on the Atlas tree.
- **Multi-Choice Nodes:** Key Notable nodes feature optional bonuses (e.g., Shrine buffs for player vs. boss).
- **Masters of the Atlas:**
    - **Jado**: Discover powerful artefacts.
    - **Hilda**: Hunt deadly monsters.
    - **Doryani**: Cleanse the corruption (**Doryani's Science**).
    - Masters can be freely switched per zone before opening a map.
