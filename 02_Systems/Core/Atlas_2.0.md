# Atlas 2.0 (Endgame)

The "Origins of Divinity" expansion introduces a major overhaul to the Atlas of Worlds, focusing on progression flexibility and targeted farming.

## 🌳 Origins of Divinity Arc
- **Progression:** A new endgame arc exploring the Precursors and "The First Edict," triggered by completing the first Atlas Tower.
- **Fixed Points:** Mechanics like **Ritual** now have guaranteed spawn locations on the Atlas (e.g., Ritual is found to the West).

## 🏰 The Fortress (Citadel)
- **New Zones:** Massive precursor structures appearing in the Atlas.
- **Map Modifiers:** Includes **Azmeri Spirits** that possess Rare monsters; spirits can move to new hosts upon the host's defeat.

## 🌳 Passive Tree & Masters
- **Full Allocation:** Players can eventually allocate every node on the Atlas tree.
- **Multi-Choice Nodes:** Key Notable nodes feature 2-3 optional bonuses (e.g., *Living Metal* or Shrine-related buffs).
- **Masters of the Atlas:** NPCs like Jado apply regional buffs. Masters can be freely switched per zone before opening a map.
