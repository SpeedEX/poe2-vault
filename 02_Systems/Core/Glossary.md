# Core Glossary

This document serves as the absolute naming authority for critical Path of Exile 2 mechanics to ensure consistency across the vault.

## 🛡️ Defensive Mechanics
- **Runic Ward**: A primary defensive layer functioning as a buffer for Life, designed to mitigate "one-shot" mechanics. Supports Runic Skills.
- **Deflection**: A defensive mechanic involving chance to mitigate damage, currently being reworked in 0.5.

## ⚔️ Offensive & Active Mechanics
- **Spirit**: Resource or thematic element used by the Spirit Walker (Huntress).
- **Cast Speed**: Affects the speed at which spells are cast.
- **Runic Skills**: Colorless, attribute/requirement-free skills that consume Runic Ward as a resource.
- **Mirror Images (Holo Form)**: Ability utilized by the Martial Artist to create illusions of the character.
- **Tame Beast**: Ability to capture Unique beast-type monsters (exclusive to Spirit Walker).

## 🗺️ Endgame & Progress
- **Waystones**: Consumable items used to open maps. Tiers: Basic, Refined (+5 level), Ancient.
- **Atlas Towers**: Fixed landmarks in the Atlas of Worlds used for progression.
- **Fortress (Citadel)**: Endgame zones containing pinnacle encounters.
- **Ezomyte Remnants**: League mechanic nodes that require runic inscription to activate monster mods.

## 💎 Crafting & Items
- **Verisium**: Ancient Kalguuran metal used for Runic Ward and forging.
- **Unique Runes**: Extracted modifiers from unique items via Orof's Legacy.
- **Masterwork Runes**: Used to upgrade runes beyond their normal maximum level.
