# Spirit Walker (Huntress Ascendancy)

The Spirit Walker is a Huntress ascendancy introduced in Patch 0.5, focused on Azmerian tier-spirits, wisps, and beast taming.

## ⚔️ Ascendancy Nodes

### Spirit Connections
- **Deer (Primordial):** Grants *Stampede* attacks.
- **Owl (Vivid):** Grants extra projectiles and increased projectile speed.
- **Bear (Wild):** Summons a powerful Bear companion that shreds enemies and debuffs them with roars.

### Holy Wisps
- **Effect:** A free Notable unlocked by combining all three spirit paths.
- **Upgrades:** 
    - **Bear:** Takes a portion of your damage and provides life regeneration.
    - **Deer:** Becomes more aggressive, leaping at enemies.
    - **Owl:** Leaves a trail of **Elevating Ground** (buffing allies).

### Idol Worship
- **Effect:** Grants significant bonuses based on **Idols** socketed in your gear.

### Natural Order (Tame Beast)
- **Effect:** Allows the character to use **Tame Beast** on Unique beast-type monsters.
- **Confirmed Targets:** *Silberfaust* (Act 3 monkey boss), *Three-headed Chimera*, *Rakkar (Frozen Claw)*.

---

## 🧪 Speculative / #unconfirmed
- **Limit:** It is unknown how many Unique beasts can be tamed or active simultaneously.
- **Scaling:** Unclear if tamed beasts scale with minion damage or player stats.
