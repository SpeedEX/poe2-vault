# Endgame Mechanics: Waystones and Map Tiers

Patch 0.5 introduces significant changes to Waystone progression and map tiering.

## 🗺️ Refined & Ancient Waystones
- **Refined Waystones:** A new type of Waystone that grants +5 monster area levels over standard Waystones.
- **Ancient Waystones:** A further tier of Waystones (speculated to be higher level/difficulty than Refined).
- **Progression Logic:** Standard Waystones (T1-T15) provide a steady progression curve. Refined Waystones offer a way to reach higher levels and reduce experience penalties for high-level characters (lvl 92+).

## 📊 Experience & Leveling
- **Penalty Reduction:** By utilizing higher-level maps (via Refined/Ancient stones), players can mitigate the severe experience penalties that currently apply after level 92-93.
- **Item Levels:** High-tier maps are necessary to acquire the new endgame base types (level 80+).

## 🧪 Speculative / #unconfirmed
- **Waystone Types:** There may be up to three types of Waystones: Basic, Refined (+5 level), and Ancient (?).
- **Refined Usage:** It is speculated that players can switch from Basic to Refined Waystones to optimize loot and experience once they reach the mid-to-late end game.
