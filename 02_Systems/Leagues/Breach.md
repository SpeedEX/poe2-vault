# Breach (Keepers of the Flame)

**Breach** has been overhauled and replaced with content inspired by the "Keepers of the Flame".

## 🔥 Mechanics: Unstable Breaches
- **Process:** Slain enemies extend the encounter time (indicator display).
- **Stabilization:** Resist the invasion long enough to stabilize the Breach and face the toughest miscreations of the **Hiveborn**.
- **Skyhives:** Purge sprawling living hive structures and **Skyhives** to collapse the Hiveborn foothold.
- **Pinnacle Bosses:** Assist NPC **Ailith** and the Keepers of the Flame.

## 🌳 Genesis Tree (Crafting)
- **Origin:** Gifted to the Keepers by **The Dreamer**.
- **Deterministic Crafting:** Unlock branches to access new **Jewelry base types** (e.g., *Fate Knot*, *Blood Gyre*, *Rage Loop*) with powerful modifiers.
- **Resources:** Fertilize the tree with **Hiveblood** (and **Wombgifts**) from slain Hiveborn.
- **Flexibility:** Points can be respec'd for free at any time.
