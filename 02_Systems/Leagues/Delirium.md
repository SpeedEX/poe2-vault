# Delirium (Tangmazu's Madness)

**Delirium** in Patch 0.5 focuses on the pursuit of the Raven Trickster and new jewel-focused crafting.

## 🌀 Mechanics: The Pursuit of Tangmazu
- **NPC:** **The Strange Voice** (Elder Madox), trapped by the trickster god **Tangmazu**.
- **Withered Willow:** Descent into the fog measures danger and madness, intensifying as players venture deeper.
- **Strange Fruit:** Consuming these draws a shroud of fog across the Atlas.
- **Simulacrum:** Overcoming the insanity in the Simulacrum earns passage to **Tangmazu's domain**.
- **Grand Mirrors:** Surface across the Atlas after the questline, allowing players to challenge Tangmazu anew.

## 💎 Distilled Emotions (Jewel Crafting)
- **Use:** **Distilled Emotions** can instill notable passive buffs onto amulets or craft specific mods onto **Jewels**.
- **Modifiers:** Can provide stats like critical strike chance, movement speed, etc.
