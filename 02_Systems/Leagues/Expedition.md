# Expedition (Kalguuran Origins)

**Expedition** in Patch 0.5 reveals its connection to the Ezomyte remnants and introduces a new island-based endgame.

## 🏝️ Mechanics: Uncharted Seas
- **Endgame Arc:** Discover Logbooks that open the way to a **vast ocean**.
- **Exploration:** Traverse mysterious islands to find Expedition Sites, dungeons, and powerful runes.
- **Bosses:** Challenge **five new Kalguuran bosses**, including **Medved**, and seek out the crater of a fallen star (Verisium meteor).

## ⚒️ Remnant Integration
- **Aldur Remnants:** Remnant content blends into Expedition encounters.
- **Crafting Inscriptions:** Remnants unlock advanced crafting inscriptions, significantly increasing challenge and reward.
- **Kingsmarch Crew:** Farrow utilizes the aid of the Kingsmarch crew to delve into long-entombed relics.
