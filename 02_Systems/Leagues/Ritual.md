# Ritual (Ritual of the Nameless)

**Ritual** has been reworked in Patch 0.5 to include a brand-new progression arc and endgame threat.

## 🩸 Mechanics: Ritual of the Nameless
- **NPC:** **Aoife**, a restless spirit searching for one to answer her call.
- **Rite of the Nameless:** Begins as a ritual encounter that swells in power. Defeating the Map Boss grants a fragment of an **effigy** (**Sylvan's Effigy**).
- **Compounding Progression:** The rite bleeds into other maps; choices made compound both difficulty and rewards.
- **New Rewards:** Baseline rewards are now guaranteed to be either **Unique items or Omens**.

## 🌲 The Wildwood & The Bodach
- **King in the Mist:** Reclaiming Aoife's flesh from the King is part of the progression.
- **Effigy Transfer:** Etch your name into the effigy to survive the ancient prison under the Wildwood.
- **The Bodach:** The final threat residing in the prison, an ancient being of darkness.
