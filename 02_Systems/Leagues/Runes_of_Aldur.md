# Runes of Aldur (League)

**Runes of Aldur** is the challenge league for Patch 0.5, focused on ancient Ezomyte technology and powerful crafting.

## 🕯️ League Mechanic: Ezomyte Remnants
- **Inscription:** Players find Remnants and inscribe them with collected **Runes**.
- **Risk vs. Reward:** Higher-tier runes increase monster difficulty but significantly boost item drop quality and rarity.

## ⚒️ Rune Forging
- A new crafting system that allows players to "forge" runes into equipment.
- **Unique Upgrading:** Specifically designed to make lower-level Unique items viable in high-level endgame content.
