# Livestream 0.5 Summary: Return of the Ancients

**Release Date:** May 29, 2026 (Early Access Update #4)
**Theme:** The return of the "Precursors" and ancient Kalguuran/Ezomyte civilizations.

## 🔑 Key Features

### 1. Runes of Aldur League
- **Mechanic:** Inscribe symbols on Ezomyte Remnants. Monsters rise upon selection; symbols dictate rewards and monster modifiers.
- **Resource:** **Verisium** is obtained from encounters for various new crafting options.
- **Runic Ward:** A new craftable defensive layer (protects Life).
- **Runic Skills:** Colorless abilities with no attribute requirements. They consume **Runic Ward** instead of Mana/Life (e.g., *Frostflame Nova*).
- **Unique Upgrading:** Reach the **Mystic Refuge** in Act 3 to upgrade low-level Uniques into powerful endgame versions.
- **Customization:** Over 100 new Runes added for gear socketing.

### 2. Atlas 2.0 & Origins of Divinity
- **Origins of Divinity:** A new core progression arc exploring the Precursors and "The First Edict."
- **Fixed Points of Interest:** The Atlas now has guaranteed locations for mechanics (e.g., Ritual is guaranteed West of the start).
- **Fortresses:** Massive citadels spawning on the Atlas, locking regions until explored.
- **Map Modifiers:** Includes **Azmeri Spirits** that possess multiple Rare monsters for increased rewards.
- **Full Allocation:** Players can eventually allocate the entire Atlas Passive Tree.
- **Multi-Choice Nodes:** Key nodes offer optional bonuses (e.g., *Living Metal* Notable: choice between Shrine buffs for player or boss).
- **Masters of the Atlas:** New NPCs (like Jado) apply special modifiers. Masters can be freely switched per zone.
- **The Arbiter:** Streamlined early progression; no longer the main boss of the core Atlas.

### 3. League Reworks

#### Breach (Keepers of the Flame)
- **Overhaul:** Replaced with content inspired by "Keepers of the Flame."
- **Unstable Breaches:** Feature a timer bar; killing monsters stabilizes the Breach for stronger spawns or bosses.
- **Genesis Tree:** Crafting system for Caster/Minion gear. Requires **Hiveblood** and **Wombgifts**. Points can be respec'd for free.
- **Pinnacle Track:** Defend NPC **Ailith** to progress toward bosses like **Tul** and **Esh**.

#### Delirium (Tangmazu's Madness)
- **Story:** Free **Elder Madox** from the Raven Trickster, **Tangmazu**.
- **Delirium Bar:** Tracks delirium level; higher levels increase difficulty and spawn special encounters.
- **Liquid Emotions:** Now used to add modifiers to **Jewels**.
- **Simulacrum:** Leads to a new Pinnacle Boss key.
- **Grand Mirrors:** Used to restart the Delirium cycle at a new Atlas location.

#### Ritual (Ritual of the Nameless)
- **Rewards:** Baseline rewards now guaranteed to be either **Unique items or Omens**.
- **Ritual of the Nameless:** Bosses are collected over several maps with increasing difficulty.
- **Endgame:** Challenge the **King in the Mist**, eventually leading to a new threat in the Wildwood.

#### Expedition
- **Integration:** Remnants are revealed to be Kalguuran. Content blends into Act 4 encounters.
- **Kingsmarch Islands:** Exploration arc to find the missing NPC **Gwennen**.
- **Boss Track:** Face **Medved** to obtain Logbooks leading to other bosses and a new Pinnacle Boss.

### 4. New Ascendancies
- **Spirit Walker (Huntress):**
    - Uses **Azmerian Wisps** (3 types) for spirit possession.
    - **Natural Order:** Allows using **Tame Beast** on Unique beast-type monsters (e.g., the Act 3 monkey).
- **Martial Artist (Monk):**
    - **Fist to Stone:** Specializes in Gloves, modifying their affixes into powerful versions.
    - **Mirror Images:** Channel to spawn an army of images that use a chosen Attack ability.

### 5. New Uniques & QoL
- **Raven's Flock:** A Unique Staff granting a Righteous Fire-like effect.
- **Atlas Search:** Long-awaited search bar function added to the Atlas.
- **Live Price Check:** `Ctrl + Alt + Click` to reach the in-game trade site instantly.
- **In-game Build Guide:** Native tool for following creator guides, including tree pathing and notes.
