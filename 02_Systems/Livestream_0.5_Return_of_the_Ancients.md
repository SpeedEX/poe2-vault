# Livestream 0.5 Summary: Return of the Ancients

**Release Date:** May 29, 2026 (Early Access Update #4)
**Theme:** The return of the "Precursors" and ancient Kalguuran/Ezomyte civilizations.
**Lore:** Guided by **Doryani**, the Vaal thaumaturge, players seek the makers of the **Third Edict** (the Precursors).

## 🔑 Key Features

### 1. Runes of Aldur League
- **NPC:** **Farrow**, a seeker of runes exploring the bond between Ezomyte tradition and Kalguuran craft.
- **Mechanic:** Inscribe symbols on Ezomyte Remnants. Monsters are empowered by the runes used (e.g., Fire, Moon).
- **Resource:** **Verisium**, used for **Runic Ward** and forging.
- **Runic Ward:** New defensive layer protecting Life.
- **Kalguuran Skills:** Colorless abilities requiring Runic Ward (e.g., *Triskelion Cascade*, *Frostflame Nova*).
- **Runeforging:** Upgrade low-level Uniques into endgame-viable versions.
- **Orof's Legacy:** Destroy Ezomyte/Kalguuran uniques to extract modifiers as socketable runes.

### 2. Atlas 2.0 & Origins of Divinity
- **The Fortress:** Reach the heart of the fortress to face **The Ordained**.
- **Fixed Points of Interest:** Guaranteed locations for mechanics across 30+ new maps.
- **Atlas Passive Tree:** Reworked for "Full Allocation" and multi-choice nodes.
- **Masters of the Atlas:**
    - **Jado**: Discover powerful artefacts.
    - **Hilda**: Hunt deadly monsters.
    - **Doryani**: Cleanse the corruption (Doryani's Science).
- **QoL**: Atlas search, market price checker, Fragment Stash Tab.

### 3. League Reworks

#### Breach (Keepers of the Flame)
- **NPC:** **Ailith**. Defend against Hiveborn invasions (Unstable Breaches with timer bars).
- **Genesis Tree:** Access new **Jewelry base types** (e.g., *Blood Gyre*, *Rage Loop*) using **Hiveblood**.
- **Skyhives:** Purge sprawling living hive structures and defeat Breachlords.

#### Delirium (Tangmazu's Madness)
- **NPC:** **Strange Voice** (Elder Madox).
- **Simulacrum**: Earn passage to the Raven Trickster's domain (**Tangmazu**).
- **Distilled Emotions**: Use to instill notables on amulets or craft modifiers onto jewels.
- **Grand Mirrors**: Surface across the Atlas after the questline is complete.

#### Ritual (Ritual of the Nameless)
- **NPC:** **Aoife** (restless spirit).
- **Rite of the Nameless:** Compounding difficulty across maps; collect fragments of **Sylvan's Effigy**.
- **Endgame:** Challenge the **King in the Mist** to enter the prison and face **The Bodach**.

#### Expedition
- **Ocean Exploration:** Use Logbooks to discover islands/dungeons in the "vast ocean."
- **Kingsmarch Crew:** Delve into relics and tombs of fallen Kalguurans.
- **Bosses:** Face 5 new Kalguuran bosses, including **Medved**.

### 4. New Ascendancies
- **Spirit Walker (Huntress):** Bound to the Stag, Owl, and Bear. **Natural Order** allows taming Unique beasts.
- **Martial Artist (Monk):** Master of illusions and spectral bells. Uses hands as weapons via **Way of the Stone Fist**.

### 5. Notable Unique Items
- **The Ordained** (Spear)
- **The Raven's Flock** (Staff - Minion RF)
- **Horror's Flight** (Gloves)
- **Sylvan's Effigy** (Shield/Relic)
- **Liminal Coil** (Wand)
- **Periphery** (Bow)
- **Brutus' Lead Sprinkler** (Revised)
- **Voices** (Jewel)
