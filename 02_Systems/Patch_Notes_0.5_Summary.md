# Patch 0.5 Summary: Return of the Ancients

**Release Date:** May 29, 2026
**Theme:** The return of the "Precursors" and ancient Ezomyte civilizations.

## 🔑 Key Features

### 1. Runes of Aldur League
- **Mechanic:** Inscribe runes onto Ezomyte Remnants to customize difficulty and rewards.
- **Crafting:** Introduction of **Rune Forging** to upgrade lower-level Uniques.
- **Runic Ward:** A new defensive layer (similar to Energy Shield but faster recharge) required for **Runic Skills**.

### 2. Atlas 2.0 (Endgame Expansion)
- **The Fortress:** Massive precursor citadels in the Atlas with Pinnacle Boss encounters.
- **Full Allocation:** The Atlas Passive Tree can now be fully completed.
- **Multi-Choice Nodes:** Notable nodes offer 2-3 specific options for strategy customization.
- **Masters of the Atlas:** Assign masters to specific Atlas regions for focused buffs.

### 3. League Reworks
- **Breach:** New **Genesis Tree** for crafting Caster and Minion gear.
- **Delirium:** **Liquid Emotions** added for upgrading Jewels.
- **Ritual:** **Ritual of the Nameless** - a 5-map sequence culminating in a new boss fight.

### 4. New Ascendancies
- **Spirit Walker (Huntress):** Wisp buffs and **Tame Beast** (capturing animal bosses).
- **Martial Artist (Monk):** Unarmed focus, **Mirror Images**, and stone-infused strikes.

### 5. Quality of Life
- **In-game Build Guide:** Native support for following creator guides.
- **Live Price Check:** `Ctrl + Alt + Click` to check market prices instantly.
