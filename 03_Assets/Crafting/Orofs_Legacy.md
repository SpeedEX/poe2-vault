# Orof's Legacy (Unique Rune Extraction)

**Orof's Legacy** is a powerful new crafting system introduced in Patch 0.5 that allows for the extraction of unique modifiers from items.

## ⚙️ Mechanics
- **Process:** Destroy an **Ezomyte** or **Kalguuran** unique item to extract one of its modifiers.
- **Result:** The extracted modifier is turned into a **Unique Rune**.
- **Usage:** The resulting rune can be socketed into a non-unique item.
- **Constraint:** The target item must share the same **Item Class** as the destroyed unique (e.g., a rune from a destroyed Shield can only be placed into another Shield).
- **Values:** Some extracted modifiers may have slightly modified values for balancing purposes.

## 🧪 Speculative / #unconfirmed
- **Mod Selection:** It is currently unclear if you can choose which modifier to extract, or if it only extracts the "unique" modifier of the item.
- **Item Compatibility:** Whether this applies to *any* mod the item grants or just specific unique-only mods is still to be determined.
- **Potential Combos:** Fubgun suggests looking at items like *Ezomyte Peak* (for Iron Stance) or *Cruel Rain* as potential extraction targets.
