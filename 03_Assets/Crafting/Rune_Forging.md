# Rune Forging

**Rune Forging** is a core crafting pillar of the Runes of Aldur league.

## ⚒️ Features
- **Unique Item Scaling:** Permits players to upgrade the base stats of lower-level Unique items to endgame tiers.
- **Rune Inscription:** Directly forge collected runes into equipment slots for custom stat bonuses.
- **Runic Ward Synergy:** Many forged runes provide bonuses to Runic Ward capacity or recovery speed.
