# Patch 0.5 Unique Items

This list tracks the new and revised Unique items introduced in Patch 0.5 "Return of the Ancients."

## ⚔️ New Weapons
- **The Ordained** (Spear): Specialized in physical and lightning damage; high crit chance. Creates "Fragments of Divinity" for empowered jumps.
- **The Raven's Flock** (Staff): Minion focus. Grants "Circle of Conspiracy" (Raven version of RF).
- **Liminal Coil** (Wand): Curse stacking focus. Grants "Winding Bolts" scaling with number of curses.
- **Periphery** (Bow): Uses Azmerian spirits for elemental damage. Summons "Azmerian Swarms" on kill.
- **Twisted Empyrean** (2H Mace): Verisium themed. Converts fire damage to cold and changes skill visuals (e.g., Glacier Spikes).
- **Ironbound** (Shield?): Mentioned in announcement.

## 🛡️ New Armour & Accessories
- **Horror's Flight** (Gloves): Chaos focus. Grants "Crushing Fear" and increases culling strike threshold.
- **Stillwater Blossoms** (Amulet): Grants Runic Ward and Arctic Blossoms on crit.
- **Sylvan's Effigy** (Relic/Shield): Related to the Ritual of the Nameless.
- **Eventide Petals** (Amulet?): Mentioned in announcement.

## 💎 Jewels
- **Voices**: Consumes 1 slot to add 2, 3, or 4 additional jewel slots.

## 🔄 Revised Uniques
- **Brutus' Lead Sprinkler**: Updated for PoE 2.
